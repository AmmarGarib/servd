# backend/src/workers

Background workers. Most are BullMQ-driven (Redis-backed queues) — a few are simple `setInterval` pollers. All gated by `ENABLE_WORKERS=true` (default off in dev, on in production).

## Workers

- `coursePacing.ts` — Polls for items past their `scheduledFireAt` and auto-fires them.
- `loyaltyTriggers.ts` — Birthday bonuses, tier promotions, expiring-points alerts.
- `loyaltyExpiration.ts` — Expires unused loyalty points after the configured TTL.
- `reservationReminders.ts` — 24h + 2h SMS reminders for upcoming reservations.
- `scheduledReports.ts` — Per-company scheduled report emails (daily sales, shift summary, low stock, **food cost**, AP aging, menu engineering), configured in Configuration → Scheduled reports. The `food_cost` email reads the same engine as Reports → Food cost variance (`modules/reports/foodCost.ts`) for the period since its last run. A schedule with no company records `failed: no company` and sends nothing.
- `fiscalConnector.ts` — Fiscal outbox: BullMQ `fiscal-connector` fast path plus a Redis-independent 30 s sweeper that sends sale / refund documents to each company's fiscal POS (the only FBR/SRB filer).
- `terminalConnector.ts` — Card-machine reconciler: every 10 s, per company with charges in flight, re-checks charge status, records approved charges and reverses void-pending ones.
- `raastConnector.ts` — Raast QR (node-cron, no Redis): every 30 s processes bank SMS the webhook's fast path missed, puts stuck `processing` claims back, and expires awaiting Raast intents; every 10 min (companies with SMS matching on) raises `raast_unconfirmed` (a typed reference no SMS confirmed) and `raast_sms_unmatched` (a bank credit no bill claimed); daily 03:15 PKT drops SMS text older than 90 days (unparsed: 30). Every write is a conditional claim, so two processes never double-apply.
- `segmentComputation.ts` — Recomputes behavioural CRM segments nightly.
- `socialPublish.ts` — Polls scheduled Meta/Instagram posts and publishes them.
- `riderDispatch.ts` — Every 30 s: times out the auto-dispatcher's unaccepted offers (3 min), then offers unassigned delivery orders placed in the last 6 h to the nearest available rider of the order's company or a fleet-agreement provider, skipping riders who declined. Both writes are claims (an offer only lands on an order still without a rider and a rider still available; a timeout never withdraws an accepted job). Hourly: prunes `rider_locations` older than 24 h.
- `giftCardExpiry.ts` — 00:10 PKT (node-cron, no Redis): marks active gift cards past `expiresAt` as `expired` and **books breakage in the same row-locked transaction** — `giftcard.expire:<card>` Dr 2080 / Cr **4200** Gift Card Breakage (other income, no sales tax) for a sold card, Cr 6060 for a promotional one (H11), in the card's company. Breakage held earlier (the card's sale not in the books, or a refused posting) is retried on every run; each card is processed once per run.
- `shrinkageTrend.ts` — 02:00 nightly: raises `shrinkage_trend` for items chronically short over the window, and (FIFO lots) `stock_shortfall` for each item whose sales of the last 7 days took more than the lots held and that no later delivery has settled (one open flag per item).
- `depreciation.ts` — **03:20 PKT daily** (node-cron, in-process running guard, `serviceClient`). Per company: asset classes + the one-off legacy 1590 reclass → `chargeThrough` the last completed PKT month (the one depreciation path; a closed month posts late into the open month with a note; a refused asset is flagged `depreciation_failed` and the rest still charge) → `releasePrepaidThrough` the same month (rent advances to 6010) → de-duplicated flags `capex_uncapitalised`, `capex_advance_overdue`, `capex_orphan_advance`, `capex_prepaid_unscheduled`, `fixed_asset_gl_mismatch`. Writes `FixedAssetRun` rows (`depreciation`, `prepaid_release`, `checks`). Daily so a month that could not post is retried; a repeat run posts nothing. See `modules/fixedAssets/README.md`.
- `intercompanyFleet.ts` — **03:40 PKT daily** (node-cron, no Redis): (1) charges every ended month of every shared-fleet agreement that has a per-trip rate above zero and was not charged yet (client Dr 6035 / Cr Due to provider; provider Dr Due from client / Cr 6990; a refusal raises `posting_failed` `ic.fleet_charge`); (2) raises one open `intercompany_mismatch` flag per company and pair whose two books disagree. Nothing is charged until the CEO sets a rate. See `modules/intercompany/README.md`.
- `cashAdvanceOverdue.ts` — **09:10 PKT daily** (node-cron, `serviceClient`): for every company with a cash request that is paid, past its due date (`paid + cashAdvanceSettleDays`, default 30) and has **no receipt uploaded** and a named holder, raises ONE `cash_request_receipts_overdue` flag in the request's company (amount = what is still open) and stamps `overdueFlaggedAt` (claimed conditionally, so two runs never flag it twice). **No money moves** — the request was expensed to its category when it was paid (H3).
