# accounting

Backend module: **accounting**. HTTP routes + business logic for the Accounting domain. Registered into Fastify by `src/index.ts` under the `/accounting` URL prefix. Everything is per company: every posting takes the company from its **source document**, every report takes `resolveTenantScope(req)`.

## Files

- `journal.ts` — **the one posting engine.** `postJournalEntry` (requires `tenantId`; asserts debits = credits; idempotent on `sourceType:sourceId`), `reverseJournalEntry`, the `ACCT` constants (every posting code lives here), `lateDatingFor` / `withLateNote`, and the **controlled-account rule**: an `intercompany` account takes only `ic.*` sources, a `head_office` account only `ho.*` (422 `INTERCOMPANY_ACCOUNT_CONTROLLED`, no override); a `fixed_asset` account (1500-1599 and 2012) takes only `fixed_asset.*` / `capex.*` unless the caller passes `controlledOverride: { reason ≥ 10 chars }` (else 422 `REGISTER_CONTROLLED_ACCOUNT`).
- `sourcePosting.ts` — `syncSourcePosting`: the keep-in-step poster for documents that are edited after posting (reverse the live revision, repost `@n`); `{ keepLiveDate }` keeps the live entry's date. `liveRevision(s)` reads which revision is live.
- `orderPosting.ts` — `order.complete`, refunds (**void split rule, TILL-R3:** a line voided on a paid, still-open order stores its exact split in the refund row's `gatewayMeta.refund.split` — line value − discount, plus the line's own GST and service charge, never fee or tip; `order.refund` books exactly that (`voidSplitOf`), and every other refund or restore values the order net of those voids (`netOfVoidRefunds`); `shareDebitLines` splits the rest; any new writer of a void refund row must pass `voidSplit`), gift-card and wallet refunds, `order.cancel_unwind` for a cancelled ticket. Sale revenue is **gross minus the delivery fee**; the fee is its own credit to **4110 Delivery Fee Income** (brand-tagged) and its GST is inside the one output-tax credit. Cost of sales is one pair per class (5010/1210, 5020/1220, 5030/1230), from the order's lot movements (`cogs.ts`). A refund takes back its share of the fee; a voided Raast receipt is not money taken (`isVoidedRaast`, `refundDueCents`).
- `shiftPosting.ts` — `shift.close`: till sales by brand (fee column carried), per-brand cost of sales from the lots, the drawer count; `summariseShiftSales` leaves voided Raast out of `cancelledPaid`.
- `cogs.ts` — `cogsLinesForOrders` (the per-class cost-of-sales lines of a set of orders), `orderCostBookedAt` (an order's cost is booked once), `PRE_FIFO_MEMO`, `unbookedSalesCostByClass`.
- `inventoryPosting.ts` — every entry made from a stock document: `postGoodsReceiptTx` (`procurement.receipt:<id>` Dr 12x0 / Cr **2015** GRNI), `postStockCount` (`inventory.count:<id>`; **opening stock found by the first count is Cr 3010 Owner's Capital**, H19), `postWasteLog` (`inventory.waste:<id>` Dr **1290** / Cr 12x0), `postPrepDraw`, `postItemReclassTx` (`inventory.item_reclass:<item>:<audit>`), `postClassReclassTx` / `postGrniOpeningTx` (the cut-over), `postGrniWriteOffTx` (Dr 2015 / Cr 5060, CEO-approved), `postFifoAlign` (`inventory.fifo_align:<tenant>` to 3020). Brand-less amounts are split by `lib/brandShare.ts` (H20). **H20 suspense:** waste, sold-line waste (`inventory/voidWaste.ts`) and spot-count differences post to **1290 Stock Differences Suspense** at once; the CEO approves each line into 5040 (waste) / 5050 (count) on Finance → Stock differences (`modules/stockDifferences/`, `stock_difference.clear:<1290 lineId>`, dated the approval day). `stockDifferenceAccount(tenantId, kind)` is the one switch; opening counts are unchanged (3010).
- `supplierPosting.ts` — supplier bills, payments, credit notes and their voids: matched receipt quantity clears **2015**; a price difference re-prices the lot (on-hand share to 12x0, used share to **5060**, split by brand); non-stock lines Dr their 6xxx; input tax Dr the **charging authority's** account; WHT Cr 2040 (`withholdingForBill` → `lib/withholding.ts`); the rest Cr 2010. A bill recorded before the company's FIFO cut-over posts the old way (Dr 1210). `billLotsForItem`.
- `pettyCashPosting.ts` — petty cash on **1011**: `postPettyCashMovementTx` (top-up, till top-up, return, spend, count), cash-request pay (`cashRequestPayLine`) and settlement (`postCashVoucherSettlementTx`, `cashvoucher.settle:<id>`), the history backfill and `pettyCashTieOut`. See `docs/cash-requests-and-petty-cash.md`.
- `expenseCategories.ts` — `PAYOUT_CATEGORIES` (the till pay-out and cash-request categories, with the 3 history-only `cogs` keys), `resolveSpendAccount`, `assertExpenseCode`.
- `prepaidReleases.ts` — `prepaid.release:<sourceType>:<sourceId>:<YYYY-MM>`: Dr 6010 (CapEx rent) or the OpEx category's account / Cr 1310, dated month end, never past the last completed month or the payment's live 1310 debit. `releasePrepaidThrough` (nightly worker + `POST /finance/prepaid-releases`), `prepaidCloseChecks`.
- `taxAuthorities.ts` — **the one authority table**: fbr 2030/1320, pra 2031/1321, srb 2032/1322, kpra 2033/1323, bra 2034/1324; province → authority; `outputTaxCodeFor`, `inputTaxCodeFor`.
- `salesTaxReturn.ts` — the return, **per authority**: output by supply class (standard, custom, zero-rated, exempt, **delivery charge**; a kept no-show deposit is 4210 other income and is not declared — only pre-H10 captures that carried tax show as `deposit_forfeit` rows), input set off only against the same authority, **1335 withheld by customers** netted per authority (`withheldByCustomersIn`), `taxPaymentsFor`. **H8 cross set-off:** federal input (1320) on the company's own food / drink stock (`CROSS_SETOFF_ELIGIBLE_CLASSES`, supplier bills and credit notes only) may be set off against a PROVINCIAL authority (`crossSetOffAllowed`); one pool per company (`federalCrossSetOffAvailable`; what is used is read from the ledger — `crossSetOffUsedCents`, every Cr 1320 in an entry with a provincial output line, linked, unlinked or never linked), FBR output still owed in 2030 takes 1320 first; the report adds `federalCrossSetOff`, `netPayableAfterCrossSetOffCents` and per authority `federalInputClaimableCents` / `crossSetOffForPeriodCents` / `netAfterCrossSetOffCents`.
- `taxPayments.ts` — `recordTaxPayment` (`salestax.payment:<id>`): Dr liability / Cr input / Cr 1335 (customer withheld set off) / Cr 1320 (`crossSetOffCents`, H8, provincial only — 422 `NO_CROSS_SET_OFF`, above the pool 422 `CROSS_SETOFF_TOO_LARGE`, checked under the 1320 advisory lock) / Cr money; authorities plus `wht` (2040) and the statutory keys `salary_tax` / `eobi` / `sessi`; `customerWithheldAvailable`, `assertWithheldNotSetOff`; `TAX_PAYER_ROLES` (admin, finance).
- `withholdingRoutes.ts` — `/accounting/withholding`: `GET/PUT /rates` (the only writer of the company's four-section WHT rates), `GET /register` (+ CSV). The rate table and resolver are `lib/withholding.ts`.
- `statutory.ts`, `statutoryRoutes.ts` — `/accounting/statutory`: salary tax **2042**, EOBI **2043**, SESSI/PESSI **2044**; accrued vs paid vs owed per month (`statutorySummary`), the one-off `statutory.reclass:<tenant>` out of 2090 / 2040 (preview, then post once), `statutoryCloseChecks`.
- `depositPosting.ts` — bank deposit drops (`postDepositDrop`).
- `loyaltyPosting.ts` — loyalty points liability.
- `reports.ts` — trial balance (`basis=inception|year`), balance sheet (retained earnings b/f + profit for the year to date, per company; fixed assets by class), P&L (other income **42xx** below operating revenue; depreciation and impairment shown), brand P&L, cash flow, GL. The shared definitions: `brandSalesRevenueCents`, `costOfSalesSplit`, `brandSalesByConcept`, `costOfSalesByConcept` (food / drinks / packaging), `isBrandSalesCode`, `isOtherIncomeCode`.
- `fiscalYear.ts` — `fiscalYearOf`, `taxYearOf`, per-company year end (`Tenant.fiscalYearEndMonth`, default June, H29), `fiscalYearsFor`, `closedAcrossYearEnd`. The roll-forward is **virtual**; nothing is posted at year end.
- `closeCheckTypes.ts` / `closeChecks.ts` — **the one period-close checklist** (`CLOSE_CHECKS`). A module adds a `CloseCheck` here; it never adds its own 409 to `POST /period-close`. Core checks: `raast`, `open_tills`, `unposted_shifts`, `unposted_orders`, `posting_failed`, `trial_balance`, `bank_unmatched`; plus HR, `statutory_reclass` / `statutory_unpaid`, the inter-company six, fixed-asset `depreciation` / `capitalisation_pending`, `card_unsettled`, `prepaid_release`, and the stock checks `grni_stale` / `inventory_postings_pending` (`reports/foodCost.ts`). The CEO closes over an overridable block with `acknowledge { keys, reason ≥ 10 }`; `trial_balance` is never overridable.
- `routes.ts` — the HTTP surface (below). `csv.ts`, `dates.ts` (PKT helpers), `taxRate.ts` — helpers.

## Routes worth knowing (all behind the `accounting` module gate)

- `GET/POST /period-close` — `GET` returns the checklist (`checks`, `months`, `isYearEnd`, `ready`); `POST` (CEO) takes `{ lockedThrough, note?, acknowledge?: { keys, reason } }`. 409 `CLOSE_NOT_READY { problems }`, 422 `NOT_OVERRIDABLE`, `OVERRIDE_REASON_REQUIRED`, `LOCK_CANNOT_REGRESS`. `chargeForClose` runs depreciation first. Audits `period.close`, `period.close_override` (+ a high `period_close_override` flag), `period.year_end_close`.
- `GET /fiscal-year`. (The year end itself is set by `PATCH /tenants/:id { fiscalYearEndMonth, reason }`, outside this gate: 409 `FY_CHANGE_AFTER_YEAR_END_CLOSE` after a year-end close without a reason.)
- `POST /journal` (CEO) — `registerOverride { reason }` for a register account; audited `journal.register_override`.
- `GET /sales-tax-return` (+ CSV with the H8 rows), `POST /sales-tax-return/payments` (`customerWithheldSetOffCents`; 422 `NO_WITHHELD_SET_OFF`, `WITHHELD_SET_OFF_TOO_LARGE`; `crossSetOffCents`; 422 `NO_CROSS_SET_OFF`, `CROSS_SETOFF_TOO_LARGE`), `GET …/payments?statutory=1`.
- `/accounting/stock-differences` (`modules/stockDifferences/`, H20): `GET /` (FINANCIALS_VIEWERS), `POST /approve { lineIds }` and `POST /:lineId/query { note }` (CEO only). Close check `stock_differences_pending` (warn, overridable); approvals inbox type `stock_difference`.
- Sub-plugins: `withholdingRoutes` at `/accounting/withholding`, `statutoryRoutes` at `/accounting/statutory`, `intercompanyRoutes` at `/accounting/intercompany`, `fixedAssetRoutes` at `/accounting/fixed-assets`, `receivableRoutes` at `/accounting/receivables`.

## Rules to keep

- **Company from the source document.** Never the operator's company for a system posting. A refused posting is flagged (`flagPostingFailed` with the document's `sourceType`) **and** gets a re-poster in `reconciliation/backfillOrders.ts`.
- **Late dating** (owner decision 6): a system or approval posting whose natural date is in a closed month posts **today** with a note naming the original date, under the same reference. A user-typed date in a closed month is 422 `PERIOD_LOCKED` and nothing is saved.
- **Money in paisa**, BIGINT for ledger columns (`lib/money.ts`); never `concept.taxRate` (use the tender rate); never an `as never` cast on a write.
- **One source per fact:** authorities in `taxAuthorities.ts`, WHT in `lib/withholding.ts`, which money account in `lib/bankAccounts.ts moneyAccountCode`, class accounts in `lib/inventoryClass.ts`, brand shares in `lib/brandShare.ts`.
- **Other income 42xx** (4200 gift-card breakage, 4210 no-show deposits kept) is never in sales KPIs, the food-cost denominator or the labour % base.
- **Reserved account ranges** (never put a template code in them): 1400-1449 head-office due-from, 1450-1499 inter-company due-from, 2100-2149 head-office due-to, 2150-2199 inter-company due-to, 6200-6249 head-office charges, 6990 the one recovery account.
